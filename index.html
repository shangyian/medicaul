How to read this data

Attained Age   Premium is based on your current age and increases each year as you age. About 3 in 4 US Medigap policies are priced this way.

Issue Age   Premium is locked at the age you purchased the policy. Doesn't rise because you age, but still rises with the carrier's annual inflation filings.

Community Rated   Same price for everyone in the rating area regardless of age. Doesn't escalate with age. Still subject to the carrier's annual inflation filings.

What this page does not show. All carriers — attained-age, issue-age, and community-rated — file annual rate increases for medical inflation and claims experience, on top of the age curve. Those increases hit everyone in the book and aren't reflected in this snapshot. A carrier that files 7% annual increases will cost dramatically more over 25 years than one that files 3%. Adding historical rate-filing data is a planned next step.

Methodology

Premiums are fetched from medicare.gov/api/v1/data/plan-compare/medigap/policies, the same JSON API that powers the official Medigap finder on medicare.gov. For each (age, gender, tobacco) tuple, the endpoint returns one monthly premium per carrier.
